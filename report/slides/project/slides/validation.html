[Member 2] To compare ideas fairly, we use five-fold cross-validation with the same folds for every step. Each fold of 292 houses is predicted by a model trained on the other 1,168, so every house is predicted once by a model that never saw it. To avoid data leakage, the median filling, scaling and one-hot encoding are refitted inside every fold. Predicting the average price gives an error of 0.408, and a simple Ridge regression gives 0.155.
