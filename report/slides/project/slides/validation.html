HOW WE MEASURE

Same five folds for every step, no leakage

  • Each fold of 292 houses is predicted by a model trained on the other 1,168.
  • Median filling, scaling and one-hot encoding are refitted inside every fold.
  • Baselines: average price 0.408, simple Ridge 0.155.
[Member 1] Thanks. To compare ideas fairly, we use five-fold cross-validation. Each fold of 292 houses is predicted by a model trained on the other 1,168, so every house is predicted once by a model that never saw it. To avoid data leakage, the median filling, scaling and one-hot encoding are refitted inside every fold. Predicting the average price gives an error of 0.408, and a simple Ridge regression gives 0.155.
