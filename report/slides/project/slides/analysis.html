[Member 1] We found a blind spot. The two outliers from our data exploration make up seventeen percent of our squared error, and both fall into the same fold, so our CV never learned from one while predicting the other. The test set has a third house of the same kind, house 2550. Because we had removed its look-alikes from training, our models priced it far above the 160 to 185 thousand dollars its look-alikes sold for.
